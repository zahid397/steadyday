# SteadyDay — pitch

## 1. Help people return to a routine

SteadyDay helps men in Bangladesh track sleep, movement and energy and choose a small next step after an interruption. This is a controlled demo for the Future Health Digital Innovation Challenge 2026.

## 2. The proposed fit is Challenge 09

A busy week can break a routine. A logging gap triggers a modest restart suggestion while keeping past entries and progress. The public Wellbeing statement, Sustaining Healthy Behaviors, fits this journey. The team dashboard selection remains to be verified.

## 3. Watch a working journey

Choose fictional Samir. Open the explanation of the restart prompt. Save a check-in, reload it, view the 14-day trend, and adjust the weekly target. Very low energy replaces the movement prompt with general support information. The demo can be exported and deleted.

## 4. Show what the technology does

FastAPI validates requests and stores each session's check-ins and goals. Docker Compose is supplied for PostgreSQL; the local Python fallback was tested with SQLite. Context rules and TF–IDF rank four fixed prompts with reasons and sources. There is a deterministic fallback. The shared browser interface runs locally; a Next.js proxy and PostgreSQL backend profile are ready for separate integration verification.

## 5. Show the evidence and limits

Local API and proxy checks cover persistence, scope, input validation, expiry and safety. Chromium confirmed the primary demo flow and narrow-width reflow. The demo does not assess disease or urgency. There are no real user records, clinical efficacy claims or validated outcomes.

## 6. Define a realistic pilot

Before a pilot, test the Docker and hosted profiles and review accessibility and health boundaries. In an opt-in pilot, measure return after gaps, repeated check-ins, prompt comprehension and selected-goal completion, with language and access barriers assessed separately. A workplace or community program could be a potential partner and buyer; confirm willingness to pay and support costs before proposing a price. No partners or customers are claimed. Android, wearables and clinical integrations are future work.
